# Late Work Policy

## Due time and grace period
Every assignment is due at 11:59 PM on its due date. A submission that arrives up to 15 minutes after the due time counts as on time; this grace period covers upload delays. The time stamp of the course site is the official submission time.

## Late penalty
A late assignment loses 10% of its points for each day or part of a day that it is late, counted from the due time. Weekends and holidays count as late days like any other day. For example, a submission that is 30 hours late is 2 days late and loses 20%.

## Maximum lateness
Late work is accepted for at most 3 days. An assignment that is more than 3 days late receives 0 points, but it should still be submitted to get feedback. The penalty does not apply to time covered by an approved extension.

## Exams and the final project
The late penalty does not apply to exams. The final project P1 is not accepted late, except when the instructor approves a documented emergency.
