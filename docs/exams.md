# Exams and Make-ups

## Exam rules
The midterm (MID) and the final exam are taken in class without notes, phones or laptops. The final exam is on December 15, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM in Room B-114.

## Missing an exam
A make-up exam is possible only for a documented emergency and only with the instructor's approval. E-mail the instructor within 48 hours after the exam with the reason. The TA desk cannot schedule a make-up.
