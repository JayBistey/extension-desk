# Extension Policy

## How many and how long
Each student may receive at most 2 extensions per term. One extension moves the due date of one assignment by 1 to 3 days. An assignment can be extended only once.

## When to ask
An extension must be requested before the original due time. A request made after the due time is not an extension: the late policy applies instead. A reason is required, but no documents are needed for an ordinary extension.

## What cannot be extended
Exams and the final project P1 cannot be extended through the TA desk. For a documented emergency (illness, a family emergency or a similar event), e-mail the instructor; the instructor decides, not the TA.

## How a request is decided
A request is reviewed by a TA. Until a TA approves it, the original due date stands and nothing changes in the gradebook. A request that is not decided within 24 hours, or by the original due time if that comes first, expires; the student may then ask again if it is still before the due time.
