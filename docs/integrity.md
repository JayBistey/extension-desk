# Academic Integrity

## Your own work
All submitted code must be written by you. You may discuss ideas with classmates, but you may not share or copy code. Using AI tools to explain concepts is allowed; submitting code that an AI tool wrote for you is not, unless the assignment says so.

## Consequences
A first integrity violation gives 0 points on the assignment and a report to the Dean of Students. A second violation gives an F in the course. The TA desk does not decide integrity cases; they go to the instructor.

## Grades and records
Only the instructor can change a grade. Staff never share one student's grades, contact details or records with another student.
