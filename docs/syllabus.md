# CS 1100 Syllabus (Brightwater College, Fall 2026, invented)

## Course and staff
CS 1100 Introduction to Programming teaches Python from variables to classes. The instructor is Dr. Rowan Hale (rowan.hale@example.edu). The teaching assistants answer at the TA desk and at cs1100-tas@example.edu. Extension requests are reviewed by a TA; make-up exams and emergencies are decided by the instructor.

## Grading weights
Five programming assignments (A1 to A5) are worth 34% together: A1 and A2 are 5% each, A3, A4 and A5 are 8% each. The midterm exam is 20%, the final project (P1) is 25%, the final exam is 15% and lab participation is 6%. Every assignment is graded out of 100 points.

## Office hours and contact
Dr. Hale holds office hours on Mondays and Wednesdays from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM in Room C-210. The TA desk is open Tuesday to Thursday from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM in the CS lab. Questions about a grade go to the instructor by e-mail within 7 days after the grade is posted.
